ATLAS Notebook & Drafting — Prototype 01
Create a NEW GitHub repository ATLAS-Notebook-Drafting. Upload index.html, manifest.json and sw.js to its root. Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main / root. Open the published HTTPS URL online once before testing offline. On Android use Chrome menu > Add to Home screen; on iPad use Safari Share > Add to Home Screen. Alternatively open index.html locally on PC (local storage behavior varies by browser).
Prototype: test with disposable notebooks. Export backups regularly. Local storage is limited and may be cleared by browser. No sync. Restore appends notebooks. Exact line starts at a fixed origin; scale locks after first mark. Protractor reports angle while dragging; squares constrain lines, not movable physical overlays. Eraser and undo are basic (eraser deletion is not undoable). Photos and text are fixed placement; no PDF import, planner or multi-page PDF export yet. Print current Letter page at actual size, no margins, headers/footers off; verify with ruler. Browser/device testing not performed.
